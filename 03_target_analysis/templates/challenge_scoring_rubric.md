# Target Analysis Challenge Scoring Rubric

Use this 100-point rubric to evaluate the independent challenge.

| Dimension | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Decision framing | 10 | Action, owner, unit, as-of date, capacity, outcome, and guardrails are explicit |
| Data quality | 15 | Defects are measured, treatment is justified, and source-to-clean counts reconcile |
| Eligibility | 20 | Rules are auditable, hard gates are separated from priority, and the funnel reconciles |
| Prioritization | 20 | Need, value, and fit logic is interpretable; tie-breaking is deterministic |
| Opportunity sizing | 10 | Counts are separated from assumptions; scenarios are calculated correctly |
| Segment and sensitivity review | 10 | Coverage is profiled descriptively and capacity tradeoffs are clear |
| Activation governance | 10 | Suppressions, minimization, versioning, approvals, expiry, and ownership are addressed |
| Communication | 5 | Recommendation is concise, decision-oriented, and limitations are prominent |

## Performance bands

- 90–100: activation-ready analytical judgment
- 75–89: strong work with limited governance or interpretation gaps
- 60–74: correct core analysis but material decision-readiness gaps
- Below 60: population, assumptions, or handoff cannot yet support action
