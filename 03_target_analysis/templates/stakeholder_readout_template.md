# Target Analysis Stakeholder Readout Template

## Decision requested

State the action, owner, population date, capacity, and approval required.

## Recommendation

Lead with the selected population, tier mix, and why this capacity is preferred.

## Population funnel

Show source, valid, eligible, prioritized, and selected counts. Name every denominator and major exclusion.

## Opportunity scenario

Separate known counts from adoption, cost, and value assumptions. Include at least one lower- and one higher-capacity scenario.

## Coverage and safeguards

Report meaningful segment selection rates, unknown-data coverage, suppressions, policy checks, and any disparity requiring review.

## Activation plan

Specify the minimum export, rule version, approvals, expiry, final suppression pass, channel owner, reconciliation, and rollback.

## Measurement plan

Define holdout design, primary outcome, guardrails, decision window, and scale criteria. Distinguish response from incremental impact.

## Limitations

State what is synthetic, assumed, descriptive, uncertain, or outside the analysis scope.
