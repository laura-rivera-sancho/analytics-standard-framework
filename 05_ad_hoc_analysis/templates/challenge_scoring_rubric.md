# Ad Hoc Analysis Challenge Scoring Rubric

| Dimension | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Decision and metric contract | 15 | Owner, action, deadline, KPI, periods, materiality, and stopping rule are explicit |
| Data validation | 15 | Grain, missingness, categories, funnel logic, and reconciliation are addressed |
| KPI-tree diagnosis | 15 | Component identity reconciles and the failing stage is isolated |
| Segment analysis | 20 | Pre-specified dimensions, denominator controls, FDR, and rate/mix decomposition are correct |
| Impact and triangulation | 10 | Baseline-based impact is correct and independent operational evidence is used |
| Interpretation | 10 | Facts, hypotheses, causal limits, and unknowns are clearly separated |
| Recommendation and handoff | 10 | Action, owner, reversibility, guardrails, and follow-up measurement are defined |
| Communication | 5 | The memo is concise, decision-first, and reproducible |

## Performance bands

- 90–100: decision-ready diagnostic judgment
- 75–89: strong analysis with limited control or handoff gaps
- 60–74: correct headline but material diagnostic or interpretation gaps
- Below 60: insufficient support for operational action
