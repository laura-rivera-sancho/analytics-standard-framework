# Ad Hoc Diagnostic Readout Template

## Decision and recommendation

State the decision, immediate action, owner, and deadline first.

## What changed

Show numerator, denominator, prior and current rates, absolute change, materiality, and comparison-period rationale.

## Where the movement occurs

Use the KPI tree, then the smallest set of pre-specified segment evidence. Distinguish within effects from mix effects and show sample-size or multiplicity controls.

## Business impact

State the baseline, denominator, value assumption, estimated gap, and why the estimate is not causal.

## Action and monitoring

Name the mitigation, rollback criteria, operational owner, customer guardrails, recovery metric, monitoring cadence, and follow-up test.

## Reproducibility

Record source snapshot, metric definition, comparison periods, code version, tested dimensions, exclusions, and unresolved-question owner.
