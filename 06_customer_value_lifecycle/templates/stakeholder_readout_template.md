# Customer Lifecycle Stakeholder Readout Template

## Decision

State the requested action, capacity, eligible population, owner, and deadline.

## Value concentration

Summarize customer and value shares by segment, including the analysis date and lookback window.

## Lifecycle movement

Show improved, stable, and declined customers plus the most decision-relevant transitions. Separate observed movement from causal explanation.

## Recommended treatment

Define the audience, scoring logic, capacity tradeoff, recommended action by segment, and operational owner.

## Measurement and guardrails

Specify randomized holdout, primary outcome, incremental value measure, contact and privacy constraints, unsubscribe and complaint guardrails, refresh cadence, and scale decision.

## Evidence boundary

Document synthetic-data status, assumptions, exclusions, uncertainty, and what evidence is still required.
