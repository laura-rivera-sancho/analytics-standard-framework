# Executive Readout Template

## 1. Business question

What decision was the experiment designed to support?

## 2. Recommendation

State the recommended action in one sentence:
- Roll out
- Iterate
- Retest/extend
- Stop/rollback

## 3. Primary result

Include:
- Control KPI
- Treatment KPI
- absolute lift
- relative lift
- confidence interval
- statistical evidence
- comparison with the minimum business-relevant effect

## 4. Guardrail assessment

Summarize whether risk, customer experience, operational cost, or other critical metrics deteriorated.

## 5. Business impact

Translate the experiment effect into a practical implication such as:
- incremental conversions
- annualized customer impact
- revenue/volume opportunity
- avoided cost
- reduced service demand

## 6. Segment insights

Report only decision-relevant segment findings. Clearly label post-hoc findings as exploratory.

## 7. Risks and limitations

Document:
- data-quality limitations
- implementation concerns
- sample/design limitations
- concurrent events
- multiple-testing considerations
- uncertainty that remains

## 8. Next actions

Define:
- rollout or follow-up test plan
- monitoring metrics
- owner
- timing
- success/failure thresholds

---

## Recommended one-slide structure

**Headline:** state the business conclusion, not the statistical method.

**Evidence:** 2-4 metrics showing primary impact and guardrail behavior.

**So what:** quantify business relevance.

**Action:** state exactly what the client/product team should do next.
