# Macro Correlation Monitor — Stakeholder Readout

## Decision

Use the monitor as an early research prompt when gold's rolling relationships with DXY or US10Y move into a materially different range. Do not use a correlation threshold as a standalone trading rule.

## What the dashboard provides

- current gold, dollar-index, and 10-year-yield observations on their latest common date
- selectable 30-, 90-, and 252-day rolling correlations
- a two-year indexed comparison to reveal divergent paths
- visible source, symbol, refresh status, transformation, and fallback metadata
- interpretation guidance beside the chart rather than in hidden documentation

## Recommended workflow

When the 90-day relationship changes sign or moves beyond approximately `±0.5`, open a research review. Check whether the 30-day measure indicates a short shock and whether the 252-day measure confirms a broader shift. Then examine independently sourced policy, inflation, liquidity, and risk-context evidence.

## Risk controls

- Correlation does not establish causation.
- Rolling estimates overlap and are not independent tests.
- Gold futures, DXY, and a yield index are carefully labeled instruments, not interchangeable proxies.
- The site identifies whether live or reference data are displayed.
- No forecast, order, position size, or buy/sell instruction is produced.
